## The State of Leonida: Rockstar's Most Expansive Map

When Rockstar Games officially unveiled **Grand Theft Auto VI**, it introduced players to the fictional state of **Leonida**, a sprawling geographic playground inspired by the state of Florida. Anchored by the neon-drenched metropolis of **Vice City**, Leonida represents the largest, most technologically intricate open-world landscape ever constructed by Rockstar Games.

Rather than relying on unverified internet rumors or fake coordinates, this guide breaks down the confirmed geography, major districts, and confirmed biomes of the GTA 6 map.

## Confirmed Cities, Districts, and Biomes

Leonida is engineered with distinct ecological and urban zones, offering unmatched environmental variety:

### 1. Greater Vice City Metropolis
The heart of the experience is Vice City, modernized with towering waterfront condominiums, buzzing nightlife strips, and deep commercial avenues:
- **Ocean Beach & Washington Beach:** Iconic art deco architecture, coastal boardwalks, and pristine white-sand shorelines.
- **Downtown Vice City:** High-density commercial financial centers with accessible skyscrapers and corporate headquarters.
- **Little Haiti & Little Havana Analogues:** Culturally rich urban neighborhoods offering authentic local flavor, street life, and unique music.
- **Port Vice City (Port Gellhorn):** A massive industrial maritime container hub facilitating international cargo and covert smuggling rings.

### 2. The Grassrivers (The Everglades Analogue)
Moving west of Vice City brings players into the untamed wilderness of the **Grassrivers**:
- **Ecosystem:** Shallow waterways, dense sawgrass prairies, and murky mangrove channels.
- **Wildlife Hazards:** Home to aggressive apex predators including alligators, venomous snakes, and diverse bird species.
- **Transportation:** Navigating the Grassrivers requires specialized watercraft such as high-powered airboats or lifted off-road trucks.

### 3. The Keys (Archipelago & Coastal Highways)
Extending south from the mainland is a picturesque chain of tropical islands reminiscent of the Florida Keys:
- **Overseas Highways:** Long, sweeping highway bridges connecting isolated communities across turquoise waters.
- **Nautical Culture:** Marina harbors, luxury yacht moorings, and coastal fishing outposts.

## Community Mapping Projects vs. Speculation

During the game's development, the dedicated GTA mapping community analyzed trailer footage, road signage, and official screenshots to assemble speculative blueprints of Leonida.

While community cartographers accurately predicted the general regional layout:
- **Debunking Rumors:** Speculative claims regarding underground government research bunkers ("Mount Kalaga") or arbitrary GPS coordinate dumps (`26.1420° N, 80.3840° W`) were fan-created concepts rather than official game features.
- **Official Scale:** Rockstar focused on ground-level density, architectural realism, and interactive interiors rather than empty landmass.

## Scale, Verticality, and Enterable Interiors

A defining evolution of the GTA 6 map is interior accessibility:
- **Living Commercial Spaces:** Players can enter dozens of authentic commercial businesses, including 24/7 convenience marts, bars, diners, pawnshops, and nightclub dance floors without loading screens.
- **Vertical Exploration:** Multi-story hotels, parking garages, and rooftop lounges provide tactical vantage points for combat and panoramic city views.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the state called in GTA 6?
The fictional state in GTA 6 is called Leonida, which is Rockstar Games' satirical take on Florida.

### How big is the GTA 6 map compared to GTA 5?
Leonida is significantly larger than GTA 5's Los Santos and Blaine County, featuring greater environmental diversity, longer road networks, and vastly more enterable interiors.

### Is the entire map unlocked from the beginning?
Yes. In line with modern Rockstar open-world design, the entire state of Leonida is open for player exploration from the start.

### Are the Florida Keys included in GTA 6?
Yes. Leonida features a sprawling coastal island chain connected by overseas highway bridges, inspired by the Florida Keys.
